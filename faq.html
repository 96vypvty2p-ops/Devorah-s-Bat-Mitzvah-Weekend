A Bat Mitzvah (literally "daughter of the commandment") is a Jewish coming-of-age ceremony marking the moment a 13-year-old girl becomes a full adult member of the Jewish community. During the Saturday morning service, Devora will lead prayers, chant from the Torah in Hebrew, and deliver a speech reflecting on its meaning. It's a milestone that's been years in the making — and we're so glad you'll be there to celebrate!

For the Saturday morning service, think synagogue-appropriate: dressy casual to cocktail attire. Covered shoulders and knees is a respectful guideline (though not strictly required). For the Saturday evening party, dress it up — cocktail attire is perfect. Have fun with it!

Welcome! You don't need any prior knowledge — just come as you are. The service is conducted in a mix of Hebrew and English, and prayer books with transliterations and translations will be available. You're welcome to participate as much or as little as feels comfortable. The congregation is warm and inclusive, and we're so glad to share this tradition with you.

The service typically runs about two hours. We'll share the exact start time closer to the date — stay tuned!
